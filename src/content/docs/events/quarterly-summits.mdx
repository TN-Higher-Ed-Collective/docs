# Quarterly Summits

## Overview

Quarterly summits are extended virtual events (2-3 hours) for deep-dive discussions on important technology topics affecting Tennessee higher ed.

## Format

- Multiple presentations or panel discussions
- Breakout sessions for specific technology domains
- Networking time for connecting with peers
- Action items and follow-up planning

## Presenting at a Summit

Interested in presenting? We welcome talks on:

- Technology implementations at your institution
- Lessons learned from major projects
- Vendor evaluations and comparisons
- Security incidents and response strategies
- Emerging technology exploration

Contact the steering committee to propose a topic.
