# Monthly Meetups

## Schedule

**When:** First Thursday of each month at 2:00 PM CT

**Where:** Discord Voice Channel — "Monthly Meetup"

**Duration:** Approximately 1 hour

## Format

Each monthly meetup features:

1. **Welcome and introductions** (5 minutes)
2. **Themed discussion or presentation** (30-40 minutes)
3. **Open Q&A and discussion** (15-20 minutes)
4. **Wrap-up and next month preview** (5 minutes)

## How to Participate

1. Join our Discord server
2. Navigate to the "Monthly Meetup" voice channel at the scheduled time
3. Come with questions and experiences to share
4. No preparation required — just show up!

## Suggesting Topics

Have a topic you'd like discussed? Post in `#general` on Discord or message any steering committee member.
