# Discord Server Guide

## Channel Structure

### Welcome & General Channels

- **#welcome** — Server rules, onboarding information, and important links
- **#introductions** — Introduce yourself to the community
- **#general** — Water cooler conversation, tech talk, career discussions
- **#announcements** — Important community updates (low traffic, high signal)

### Technology Domain Channels

- **#active-directory** — AD management, Group Policy, Azure AD integration
- **#vmware** — vSphere, ESXi, virtualization, clustering
- **#networking** — Switches, routers, VLANs, firewalls, VPNs
- **#storage** — SAN, NAS, backup solutions, disk management
- **#security** — Cybersecurity, compliance, patching, incident response
- **#monitoring** — Grafana, Prometheus, SNMP, alerting systems
- **#automation** — PowerShell, Python, Ansible, scripting
- **#cloud-services** — Azure, AWS, hybrid cloud, migrations
- **#databases** — SQL Server, MySQL, PostgreSQL
- **#helpdesk** — Ticketing systems, service desk operations
- **#web-services** — Web servers, application hosting, CMS platforms

### Community Channels

- **#wins** — Celebrate your successes!
- **#lessons-learned** — Share what didn't work
- **#vendor-discussions** — Vendor experiences and recommendations
- **#job-postings** — Internal opportunities across Tennessee institutions
- **#random** — Off-topic fun

### Voice Channels

- **General Voice** — Drop in anytime for quick conversations
- **Monthly Meetup** — First Thursday of each month at 2 PM CT
- **Office Hours** — Informal help sessions

## Discord Etiquette

### Do's

- Search before asking — someone may have already answered
- Use threads for detailed discussions
- Share context when asking questions — what have you tried?
- Give credit when sharing others' work
- Use code blocks for scripts and configs (triple backticks)
- Use reactions to show agreement/appreciation

### Don'ts

- Share credentials or sensitive institutional data
- Post in multiple channels (cross-posting)
- Use @everyone or @here (reserved for moderators)
- Engage in vendor promotion or sales
- Share pirated software or license keys

## Getting Help in Discord

1. Choose the right channel for your question
2. Provide context: What are you trying to do? What have you tried?
3. Share relevant error messages or screenshots
4. Be patient — members respond when they can
5. Follow up with the solution once you find it (helps others!)

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter problematic behavior:

1. Take a screenshot if relevant
2. Note the date/time and channel
3. DM any steering committee member
4. We take all reports seriously and handle them confidentially
