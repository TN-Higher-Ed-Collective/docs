# TN HETC Charter

## Article I: Name and Purpose

**Name:** Tennessee Higher Ed Tech Collective (TN HETC)

**Purpose:** To connect IT professionals at Tennessee's community colleges and TCATs through shared technology knowledge, collaborative problem-solving, and open-source resources.

## Article II: Membership

### Eligibility

Membership is open to all IT professionals working at Tennessee community colleges and Tennessee Colleges of Applied Technology (TCATs), including but not limited to:

- Systems administrators
- Network engineers
- Help desk technicians
- IT directors and managers
- Security professionals
- Database administrators
- Application administrators
- Student technology workers

### Joining

There is no formal application process. Membership begins by joining the community Discord server and/or participating in GitHub repositories.

### Expectations

All members agree to:
- Follow the [Code of Conduct](/community/code-of-conduct)
- Treat fellow members with respect and professionalism
- Contribute when able (sharing knowledge, code, or experiences)
- Maintain confidentiality about institutional-specific information shared in private channels

## Article III: Governance

### Steering Committee

A volunteer steering committee provides guidance and direction for the community. The committee:

- Consists of 3-7 members from different institutions
- Meets monthly to discuss community direction
- Makes decisions by consensus when possible, majority vote when needed
- Serves one-year terms with no limit on re-election

### Domain Champions

Volunteer subject matter experts who help curate content and guide discussions in their area of expertise. Champions:

- Are recognized experts in their technology domain
- Help organize and maintain relevant GitHub repositories
- Moderate related Discord channels
- May organize focused discussions or presentations

## Article IV: Activities

### Regular Activities

- **Monthly Meetups:** First Thursday of each month at 2 PM CT via Discord voice
- **Discord Community:** Ongoing asynchronous collaboration and discussion
- **GitHub Repositories:** Shared scripts, configurations, and documentation

### Periodic Activities

- **Quarterly Summits:** Longer virtual sessions for deep-dive topics
- **Annual Gathering:** In-person or hybrid event for community building

## Article V: Licensing and Intellectual Property

All community-created content and code is licensed under the MIT License unless otherwise specified by the contributor. Contributors retain credit for their work.

## Article VI: Amendments

This charter may be amended by a two-thirds vote of active steering committee members, with notification to the broader community at least 14 days before the vote.
