# Core Values

These five values guide everything we do at TN HETC — from how we communicate to how we make decisions.

## 1. Collaboration Over Competition

**Success for one is success for all.**

We believe that when one institution improves its technology capabilities, the entire Tennessee higher education system benefits. We actively share solutions, configurations, and knowledge — not because we have to, but because it makes all of us stronger.

*In action:* When you solve a tricky Active Directory issue, you share the solution on Discord. When your colleague at another institution faces the same problem, they find your answer and save hours of troubleshooting.

## 2. Transparency

**Share what works and what doesn't.**

We're honest about our successes and our failures. Sharing what didn't work is just as valuable as sharing what did — it saves others from making the same mistakes. We don't hide behind polished presentations; we share real experiences.

*In action:* A member posts in #lessons-learned about a storage migration that went sideways, including what they'd do differently. Other members learn from the experience without suffering the consequences.

## 3. Accessibility

**Free, open, welcoming to all skill levels.**

This community is for everyone — from the student worker on the help desk to the IT director with 20 years of experience. No question is too basic, no topic too advanced. We meet people where they are and help them grow.

*In action:* A new help desk technician asks a "basic" question about Group Policy in Discord. Instead of dismissing it, experienced members provide clear, patient answers and point to resources for learning more.

## 4. Practical Impact

**Real solutions for real problems.**

We focus on practical, actionable solutions — not theoretical discussions. Our shared repositories contain working scripts, tested configurations, and proven approaches. Everything we create is designed to be used, not just admired.

*In action:* Instead of debating the best monitoring approach in abstract terms, a member shares their working Grafana dashboard configuration with instructions for adapting it to other environments.

## 5. Continuous Learning

**Growing together through shared experience.**

Technology changes constantly, and none of us can keep up alone. By learning together — through monthly meetups, shared documentation, and daily Discord discussions — we all stay current and grow professionally.

*In action:* Monthly themed discussions tackle emerging topics like cloud migration strategies, zero-trust security, or AI tools for IT operations. Members share their experiences and learn from each other's approaches.
