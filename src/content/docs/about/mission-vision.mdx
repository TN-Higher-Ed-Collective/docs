# Mission & Vision

## Our Mission

To connect Tennessee's colleges and universities through shared technology knowledge, collaborative problem-solving, and open-source resources — empowering IT professionals to learn from each other's experiences and accelerate innovation across our institutions.

## Our Vision

A thriving community where Tennessee's higher education institutions lead through collaboration, with every IT professional having access to peer expertise, proven solutions, and a supportive network that eliminates duplication of effort and accelerates technology adoption statewide.

## Why TN HETC Exists

IT professionals at Tennessee's colleges and universities face similar challenges daily — managing Active Directory, configuring networks, deploying VMware environments, securing systems, and supporting end users. Yet most of us solve these problems in isolation, unaware that a colleague at another institution may have already developed an elegant solution.

**The Problem We're Solving:**

- **Knowledge Isolation** — Solutions developed at one institution rarely reach others
- **Duplication of Effort** — Multiple institutions independently solving the same problems
- **Limited Peer Networks** — Few opportunities to connect with IT peers at other Tennessee institutions
- **Vendor Dependency** — Without peer support, institutions rely heavily on vendor-provided solutions

## How We're Different

TN HETC is **not** an official channel of TBR, the UT System, or any other governing body. We are a grassroots, practitioner-led community that:

- Focuses on **practical, hands-on** technology solutions
- Operates **informally** without bureaucratic overhead
- Values **doing over discussing** — we share working code, not just ideas
- Welcomes **all skill levels** — from student workers to IT directors
- Remains **vendor-neutral** — we share honest experiences, not sales pitches
