# Governance Model

TN HETC operates with a lightweight, practitioner-led governance model designed to keep things moving without unnecessary bureaucracy.

## Steering Committee

### Structure

The steering committee consists of 3-7 volunteer members from different institutions who provide direction for the community.

### Responsibilities

- Set overall community direction and priorities
- Resolve conflicts and enforce the Code of Conduct
- Approve new repositories and major initiatives
- Organize quarterly summits and annual gatherings
- Review and merge significant documentation changes
- Represent the community to external stakeholders

### Current Members

- **Aaron Sachs** — Chattanooga State Community College (Founder)
- *Additional members to be added as the community grows*

### How to Join

Interested in joining the steering committee? We look for members who:

1. Have been active participants for at least 3 months
2. Represent institutions not already on the committee
3. Are willing to commit 2-4 hours per month
4. Demonstrate alignment with our core values

Express your interest to any current committee member on Discord.

## Domain Champions

### Role

Domain champions are recognized subject matter experts who volunteer to help curate content and guide discussions in specific technology areas.

### Current Domains

- Active Directory & Identity Management
- VMware & Virtualization
- Networking & Infrastructure
- Storage & Backup
- Security & Compliance
- Monitoring & Automation
- Cloud Services
- Help Desk & Service Management

### Becoming a Champion

1. Demonstrate expertise through community participation
2. Express interest to the steering committee
3. Commit to reviewing pull requests in your domain
4. Help moderate relevant Discord channels
5. Organize at least one focused discussion per quarter

## Decision Making

### Day-to-Day Decisions

Community members and domain champions make routine decisions independently — adding scripts, answering questions, organizing discussions.

### Significant Decisions

The steering committee handles decisions that affect the broader community:

- Adding or removing repositories
- Changes to governance or charter
- Code of Conduct enforcement
- Partnerships or external collaborations

### Process

1. **Proposal:** Any member can propose changes via Discord or GitHub
2. **Discussion:** Open discussion period (minimum 7 days for major changes)
3. **Decision:** Steering committee decides by consensus or majority vote
4. **Communication:** Decisions are shared in #announcements

## Getting Involved in Leadership

TN HETC thrives on volunteer participation. Here's how to get more involved:

1. **Start participating regularly** in Discord and GitHub
2. **Contribute consistently** — share knowledge, review code, help others
3. **Volunteer for tasks** — organize a meetup topic, write documentation
4. **Express interest** in domain champion or steering committee roles
5. **Mentor newer members** — help onboard newcomers

There's no formal hierarchy beyond what's described here. We value contribution over titles.
