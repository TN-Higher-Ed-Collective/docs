# VMware & Virtualization Best Practices

Best practices for VMware vSphere and virtualization at Tennessee community colleges and TCATs.

> This page is a living document. Contribute your knowledge by submitting a pull request or sharing in the `#vmware` Discord channel.

## Common Challenges

- ESXi host management and patching strategies
- VM template standardization across campuses
- Disaster recovery and business continuity planning
- Storage optimization for virtual environments
- Migration planning (on-prem to hybrid cloud)
